The circuit uses an Arduino Uno to control 11 LEDs connected to digital pins 2–12. Each LED is connected with a resistor to protect it from excess current.

The Arduino sends HIGH and LOW signals to turn the LEDs ON and OFF in a sequence. The LEDs light from both ends toward the center, the middle LED blinks twice, and then the pattern reverses outward.

This circuit helps beginners learn Arduino programming, digital outputs, and LED control.
