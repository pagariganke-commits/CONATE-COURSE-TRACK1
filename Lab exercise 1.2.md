When a pin sends a HIGH signal (5V), the connected LED lights up. When the signal is LOW (0V), the LED turns off. The resistors are used to limit current and protect the LEDs from damage.

This circuit is commonly used for learning basic electronics and Arduino programming. It helps beginners understand digital outputs, LED control, and simple circuit connections. Applications include LED blinking patterns, traffic light simulations, and decorative lighting projects.
