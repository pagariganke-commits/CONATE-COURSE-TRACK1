This circuit uses an Arduino UNO, a 7-segment display, and two push buttons connected on a breadboard. The Arduino powers the circuit and controls the display through digital pins. Resistors are used to protect the display and stabilize the buttons. When a button is pressed, the Arduino changes the number shown on the 7-segment display.
